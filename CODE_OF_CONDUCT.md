# Community code of conduct / 社区行为准则

TimeMaster welcomes respectful, evidence-based participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Discuss code, behavior, design, and verifiable facts; do not attack or harass people.
- Do not discriminate, threaten, spam, or publish another person's private information.
- Never post real user data, expense records, precise locations, credentials, or vulnerability details in public discussions.
- Give constructive feedback, assume good faith where reasonable, and correct mistakes transparently.
- Use the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive, discriminatory, unsafe, spammy, or off-topic content and may restrict participation after repeated or serious violations. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented where doing so does not expose private or security-sensitive information.

---

时间大师欢迎不同背景和经验水平的参与者，以尊重他人、基于证据的方式协作。

- 围绕代码、行为、设计和可验证事实讨论，不攻击或骚扰个人。
- 不歧视、威胁、刷屏或公开他人的隐私信息。
- 不在公开讨论中发布真实用户数据、费用记录、精确位置、凭据或漏洞细节。
- 提供建设性反馈，在合理范围内善意理解他人，并透明纠正错误。
- 漏洞请使用 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 说明的私密渠道。

维护者可以编辑、隐藏或移除辱骂、歧视、不安全、垃圾或偏离项目主题的内容，并可对重复或严重违规者限制参与。只要不会暴露隐私或安全敏感信息，处理决定应保持适度并留下说明。
